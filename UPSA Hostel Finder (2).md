## Other private rooms and apartments

In Madina, East Legon and North Legon, shared rooms (3 to 4 in a room) usually cost GH₵ 3,500 to GH₵ 6,000 or more per semester, depending on air conditioning, self-contained rooms and how reliable the water is. Private one-in-a-room apartments run from about GH₵ 12,000 for 6 months to GH₵ 22,000 or more per year.

UPSA figures are from its published hostel fees for the 2nd semester of 2025/2026, for continuing students (levels 200 to 400). Fees for new students are not listed in that notice. Private hostel figures come from public listings and adverts and can change. Confirm the room, price and rules with the hostel before you pay anything.
